- Keep unwanted styles/vocals in the separate Exclude/negative field when available; do not mix a large negative list into the positive style prompt.
- For v6, prefer specific musical behaviors and arrangement intent over piles of adjectives. Treat community syntax tricks as probabilistic, not hidden guaranteed commands.
- Front-load the most important genre, vocal identity and groove behavior; do not hide them behind long harmony or production prose.
- Use a structured production brief rather than a long tag cloud. Keep critical requirements conceptually separated and avoid conflicting descriptors.
- When exact recurring singer identity matters, prefer an approved Suno Persona/Voice/reference workflow; text prompts mainly stabilize behavior and phonation coordinates.
- Use clear section tags in the Lyrics field; tags organize structure, but do not assume every community-only modifier is deterministic.
- Record generated-result feedback (KEEP/REGEN plus failure reason) and favor recipes that repeatedly work for the user's own channels over generic internet recipes.
